## Monday, 6:12 in the morning

Let me take you to the receiving bay on Monday 22 June.

[The warehouse truck backs into Store 014's receiving bay at 6:12, twelve minutes into its 06:00–06:30 slot](../data/#logistics-records). [It carries two pallets: 16 cases of 5 kg atta, 9 of 1 kg atta, 14 of rice and 5 of sugar](../data/#logistics-records).

[The clerk scans the two pallet labels, and the store's books go up by exactly what the warehouse's shipping notice lists](../logistics/technical/#tests-that-pass), including 64 bags of 5 kg atta. By every record, the delivery is on time and in full.

Now look inside. [On the first pallet, the 16 bales on line 1 hold 1 kg packs, 160 of them](../data/#logistics-records). [The 5 kg bags the notice promised are still in the warehouse](../logistics/technical/#mirror-pairs).

## Five readings of one delivery

Nobody here is careless. Each data product reads the records in front of it.

- **The OTIF dashboard.** [The store's scorecard says 88% of June's 25 deliveries arrived in the slot and in full, and the warehouse's own report says 92%](../logistics/technical/#otif-reconciled). The 22 June delivery counts as perfect, and [the truck made the slot on 23 of 25 mornings](../logistics/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer).
- **The data tests.** [All ten pass](../logistics/technical/#tests-that-pass), because every notice line was confirmed by a pick scan, and every line was received as the notice listed it.
- **The inventory reports.** [The warehouse counted 16 cases short in its 1 kg atta slot on Tuesday and 16 cases over in its 5 kg slot on Thursday, and closed each](#four-corrections-one-mistake).
- **The store's loss report.** [A count on Friday 26 June, ordered after complaints, found the books at 63 bags of 5 kg and the bay empty, and 157 more 1 kg packs than the books showed](#four-corrections-one-mistake).
- **The AI assistant.** Given the shipping schema, [the series' scripted assistant calls the 22 June delivery on time and in full, and the missing atta possibly theft](../logistics/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer).

## What actually happened

Let us go back to 03:00 that morning.

[The picker went to slot A-07-03 for 16 cases of 5 kg atta, scanned the slot's label, and took 16 bales from A-07-04 next to it: the same brand's 1 kg packs](../logistics/technical/#the-trace). [The scan confirmed where the picker stood, not what the picker took](../logistics/technical/#the-records).

[Every record after that was written from the pick confirmation](../logistics/technical/#the-records). The shipping notice copied it, the pallet label pointed to the notice, and the goods receipt copied the notice.

<DocumentsAgree />

[The store's books now held 64 bags of 5 kg that did not exist, while 160 packs of 1 kg sat in the backroom off the books](../data/#logistics-records). [The 5 kg shelf ran out at 3:30 on Tuesday afternoon](../data/#logistics-records), and [for three nights the reorder system saw 63 bags on the books against a reorder point of 40 or 41, and ordered nothing](../data/#logistics-records). [The shelf stayed empty for 51.5 open hours, until Friday's count zeroed the books and Saturday's truck refilled the bay](../data/#logistics-records).

<ShelfAfterPallet />

[Customers wanted 72 bags of 5 kg during the gap and could not buy them](../data/#logistics-records): 29 of those became trips to another store, 16 a different pack size and 12 another brand, 9 shoppers came back later, and 6 bags were never bought.

**Every record was right about the paperwork, and none of them looked inside the pallet.**

## Why the data could not say so

[Records written from one source always agree](../who-saw-what/#join-the-records-two-at-a-time), so tests that compare them can only pass. This is the industry's design and not a shortcut. [The SSCC on a pallet label identifies the pallet and points to the shipping notice](https://gs1uk.org/support-training/help-to-use-and-implement-gs1-standards/logistics-labels-and-ssccs), and not to what is on the pallet.

"In full" is a fact about the cases on a pallet, and [none of June's 30 pallets was weighed or case-scanned](../who-saw-what/#who-saw-what).

This is the normal case. As per WERC, [best-in-class order-picking accuracy is 99.68%](https://www.yale.com/globalassets/coms/yale/north-america/documents/white-papers/snack-drawer/YALE-1520_WERC-DC-Metrics-infographic.pdf), so even the best warehouses get about one order in 300 wrong. And in [Auburn University's 2017 study](https://www.scdigest.com/assets/reps/2018_ASN_Study.pdf), retail warehouses physically checked an average of 57% of incoming orders.

## Four corrections, one mistake

[The mis-pick left four records wrong, two in each building](../logistics/technical/#corrections-in-both-buildings), and each was found and fixed on its own, days apart.

<Corrections />

[Across the company the four corrections net to about ₹80 at cost, against ₹44,648 gross](../logistics/technical/#corrections-in-both-buildings), because the mistake moved stock from one item to its sibling and from one building to the other. A net that small is why nobody chased them.

## What domain knowledge changes in the data work

Each change uses only the records, and each is scored against the answer key.

- **Ask which records are independent.** [A test that asks whether anything other than the pick checks each pallet fails for all 30 of June's pallets](../logistics/technical/#tests-that-pass).
- **Pair corrections before you explain them.** [June's records hold five mirror pairs](../logistics/technical/#mirror-pairs). Four join the atta corrections, and the fifth is toned and full-cream milk at the month-end count, the till's scan errors from the retail story.
- **Trace the pair to the pick.** [A query for 16-case picks of 5 kg from slot A-07-03 returns one row, line 1 of the 22 June shipment](../logistics/technical/#the-trace). [The warehouse's pair was complete at 10:00 on Thursday 25 June, a day before the store's count](../logistics/technical/#the-trace).
- **Reconcile the KPI with a count.** [Taking out the one receipt the next count contradicted brings OTIF from the records to 84%](../logistics/technical/#otif-reconciled), the answer key's number.
- **Give the assistant a domain layer.** [With definitions, the pick confirmations, the pallets and the warehouse's corrections, the same assistant answers seven questions of seven, against one with the schema alone](../logistics/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer).

## What would have helped

[The demo](../demo/#logistics) tries three changes from 1 June, with the same customers and the same trucks.

- **Scan every case at pick.** [The scanner reads the bale's barcode instead of the slot's label, and rejects a 1 kg bale on a 5 kg line at 03:00](../demo/#logistics). [Every record downstream is true, and the scorecard and the shelf agree at 88%](../demo/#logistics).
- **Weigh every pallet at dispatch.** [The first pallet weighs about 564 kg against the 724 kg its notice implies, 22% light](../demo/#logistics). [The line is re-picked, the truck reaches the store at 06:42, outside its slot, only one customer all week misses a 5 kg bag, and the scorecard falls to 84%](../demo/#logistics).
- **Scan every case at the store.** [The clerk finds the 1 kg bales and books what arrived, no customer finds the 5 kg shelf empty, and the scorecard falls to 84%](../demo/#logistics), because it now records what arrived.

Two of the three fixes make the headline number worse.

**A measure that reads only the sender's records gets better when you stop checking.**
