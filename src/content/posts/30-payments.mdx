## Saturday, 6:48 in the evening

It is the Saturday evening rush, and the queues at Store 014 are long. [A customer's basket comes to ₹1,064.47. She scans the QR code at the till, approves the payment on her phone, and her bank takes the money](../data/#payments-records).

[The till waits 30 seconds, hears nothing back, and shows "Payment failed: no response"](../data/#payments-records). The cashier asks for another way to pay, and she pays by card.

[87 seconds after she approved the UPI payment, the store's bank confirms the credit](../data/#payments-records). Nobody at the till sees it. [She phones the store on Tuesday, and gets ₹1,064.47 back on the following Sunday, eight days after her shopping](../data/#payments-records).

## Five readings of one payment

Nobody here is careless. Each data product reads the records in front of it.

- **The payments dashboard.** [The tills approved 95.9% of June's UPI attempts](../payments/technical/#rates-by-the-hour). On Saturday 20 June [approvals fell to 69% and 67% in the 18:00 and 19:00 hours](../data/#payments-records), and between 18:20 and 19:50 [cashiers asked for another payment 30 times](../data/#payments-records). [The acquiring bank's switch was slow, and the incident was closed](../data/#payments-records).
- **The data tests.** [All ten pass](../payments/technical/#tests-that-pass), because every bill has exactly one approved payment, and the settlements add up.
- **The reconciliation report.** [On Saturday there were 607 UPI credits against 597 approved payments, and on Sunday 670 against 663](../data/#payments-records). [The 17 credits with no approved payment behind them go into a suspense account until someone claims them](../payments/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer).
- **Customer support.** [26 customers complain about payments in June](../data/#payments-records). [The routine is to ask for a screenshot, check it against the provider's report, and refund](../data/#payments-records).
- **The AI assistant.** Given the till's schema, [the series' scripted assistant says nobody paid twice, and calls the store's 0.76% technical decline rate acceptable](../payments/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer).

## What actually happened

[On Saturday evening the acquiring bank's UPI switch slowed down, and between 18:20 and 19:50 about a third of UPI attempts at Store 014 got no answer within the till's 30 seconds](../data/#payments-records). Put differently, [the tills gave up on 28 of 82](../data/#payments-records).

Now here is the part many of us miss. A slow answer is not a failed payment. [When the customer has been debited and the answer is late, UPI settles the payment afterwards: the merchant's bank either confirms the credit or the money goes back](https://www.npci.org.in/PDF/npci/upi/circular/2017/Circular-17-Debit-Reversals-and-Deemed-Approval_0.pdf). [Since 2019, RBI has required that money taken for a merchant payment that is never confirmed comes back within five days, with ₹100 a day in compensation after that](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=11693).

In those 90 minutes, [15 customers paid twice, two of them three times: 17 extra charges, ₹7,825.69](../data/#payments-records).

Across June, [24 customers paid twice: 26 extra charges, ₹13,366.06](../data/#payments-records), the rest of them on ordinary days. [The store refunded a double payment only when the customer complained](../data/#payments-records), and when June closed [four extra charges, ₹2,846.06, were still sitting in suspense](../data/#payments-records).

**The till said failed, the bank said paid, and the customer paid twice.**

## Why the data could not say so

[A till that hears nothing back never learns the payment's reference number, the RRN](../payments/technical/#the-key-that-is-not-there), so there is nothing to join on. [The provider's report has the RRN and a success, and the till has a failure and no RRN](../payments/technical/#the-records).

Every record is right about what it saw, and [the data tests check that the records agree, which they do](../payments/technical/#tests-that-pass). [The only record of the double charge was in the customer's bank statement](../payments/technical/#the-records).

I believe this is not rare. [UPI processed 24.07 billion transactions in September 2026](https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/upi-processes-2407-billion-transactions-worth-rs-2937-lakh-crore-in-september), and [on 12 April 2025 its success rate fell to about 50% for nearly two hours](https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/upi-outage-attributed-to-surge-in-api-calls-from-banks-says-npci).

## What domain knowledge changes in the data work

Each change uses only the records, and each is scored against the answer key.

- **Treat a timeout as a question.** [Matching each of June's 48 timeouts to the provider row with the same amount, started within five seconds, matches every one](../payments/technical/#the-key-that-is-not-there): 26 were credited late, 6 were debited and returned, and 16 never left the customer's account.
- **Then the double charges are a query.** [It finds all 26 extra charges, with no false alarms](../payments/technical/#the-extra-charges). [Run each morning on the provider's file, it finds them a median 0.6 days after the debit, against 2 days for complaints, and three customers never complained](../payments/technical/#the-extra-charges).
- **Measure the wait from the debit.** [From the complaint, refunds took a median 3 days, from the extra debit 4.9, and 10 of the 22 refunds took longer than five days](../payments/technical/#clocks-that-start-at-the-harm).
- **Read rates by the hour, with timeouts counted.** [June's technical decline rate, 0.76%, counts no timeouts at all, and in the 18:00 and 19:00 hours on 20 June the tills got no answer on 25% and 26% of attempts](../payments/technical/#rates-by-the-hour).
- **Give the assistant a domain layer.** [With definitions, the provider's report and two views built from them, the same assistant answers seven questions of seven, against one with the till's schema](../payments/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer).

## What would have helped

[The demo](../demo/#payments) tries two changes from 1 June, with the same customers, baskets and banks.

- **Wait for the status check.** [NPCI's rules allow the first check on a payment's status 90 seconds after it](https://www.medianama.com/2025/04/223-npci-new-guidelines-upi-outage/). [If the till waits for it before asking for another payment, double charges in June fall from 24 customers to 9](../demo/#payments), but during the slowdown the tills spend 99 minutes taking payment instead of 77.
- **Put the bill ID on every UPI request.** [Each credit then names its bill, and the next morning's match finds and refunds every extra charge: the median wait falls from 4.9 days to 0.7, nothing is left in suspense, and complaints fall from 26 to 12](../demo/#payments).

The cheaper fix is in the back office, and not at the till.

**A payment that carries a reference to what it is for can always be matched to it.**
