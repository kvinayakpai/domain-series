When does the refund clock start, at the debit or at the complaint? Refund turnaround measures how long customers wait for their money back. For failed payments, [RBI sets the clock: if a customer is debited for a payment a merchant never confirms, the money must be back within five days of the transaction, with ₹100 a day in compensation after that](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=11693).

## What the report said

[Store 014's support team measured refunds from the complaint: a median 3 days](../payments/#what-domain-knowledge-changes-in-the-data-work). [The routine was to ask for a screenshot, check it against the provider's report, and refund](../payments/#five-readings-of-one-payment).

## What happened

Customers lost the money at the debit, and not at the complaint. [Measured from the extra debit, refunds took a median 4.9 days, and 10 of the 22 refunds came later than five days](../payments/#what-domain-knowledge-changes-in-the-data-work). [When June closed, four extra charges, ₹2,846.06, were still unrefunded](../payments/#what-actually-happened), and [three of those customers never complained](../payments/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer).

[The customer charged twice at 6:48 on a Saturday phoned on Tuesday, and got her ₹1,064.47 back on the following Sunday, eight days after her shopping](../payments/#saturday-648-in-the-evening).

**Support said 3 days, and the customer waited 4.9.**

## Why the number missed it

A clock that starts at the complaint leaves out the days before the customer noticed, and leaves out completely the customers who never call. [Support measures from the complaint, and the customer lost the money at the debit](../payments/technical/#clocks-that-start-at-the-harm).

## How to catch it

Start the clock at the debit, and count refunds made without a complaint. [Matching timeouts to the provider's report each morning finds every extra charge a median 0.6 days after the debit](../payments/#what-domain-knowledge-changes-in-the-data-work), so the store can refund before anyone calls.

[With the bill ID on every UPI request, the median wait falls to 0.7 days and nothing is left in suspense](../payments/#what-would-have-helped). RBI's five days apply to failed payments and not double charges, so [the series uses them as the store's own yardstick](../payments/technical/#clocks-that-start-at-the-harm).
