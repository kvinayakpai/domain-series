The technical decline rate is the share of payment attempts that fail for technical reasons, such as a bank or switch error. [NPCI's chief executive put UPI's technical declines at 0.7–0.8% of transactions in November 2024](https://inc42.com/buzz/technical-glitches-on-upi-have-fallen-below-1).

## What the dashboard said

[Store 014's technical decline rate for June was 0.76% of UPI attempts](../payments/#five-readings-of-one-payment), in line with UPI as a whole. Asked whether that was acceptable, [the series' scripted AI assistant, given the till's schema, said yes](../payments/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer).

## What happened

[On Saturday 20 June the bank's UPI switch slowed down, and between 18:20 and 19:50 about a third of UPI attempts at Store 014 got no answer within the till's 30 seconds](../payments/#what-actually-happened): [the tills gave up on 28 of 82](../payments/#what-actually-happened), and [15 customers paid twice](../payments/#what-actually-happened).

Slowdowns across the whole system happen too: [on 12 April 2025, UPI's success rate fell to about 50% for nearly two hours](https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/upi-outage-attributed-to-surge-in-api-calls-from-banks-says-npci).

## Why the number missed it

It missed in two ways. [Timeouts are not counted as technical declines at all](../payments/#what-domain-knowledge-changes-in-the-data-work). And a month averages away an hour: [in the 18:00 and 19:00 hours on 20 June, the tills got no answer on 25% and 26% of attempts](../payments/#what-domain-knowledge-changes-in-the-data-work).

## How to catch it

Count timeouts, and read the rate by the hour. [Flagging any hour with three or more timeouts and a share more than ten times the month's picks out exactly two hours: 18:00 and 19:00 on Saturday 20 June](../payments/technical/#rates-by-the-hour).

Watch response times as well: [from 16 June 2025, NPCI cut the time allowed for a UPI payment response from 30 seconds to 15](https://www.medianama.com/2025/06/223-npci-upi-changes-transaction-speed-fraud-prevention/).

## If an AI agent reads it

An agent watching the monthly rate would never raise an alert. Given the timeouts and the provider's report, [the same scripted assistant answered that the month looked fine, but the tills got no answer on a quarter of attempts in two hours that evening](../payments/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer).
