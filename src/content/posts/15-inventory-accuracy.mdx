Inventory record accuracy asks how often the stock in the system matches what is physically in the store, and [in a study of nearly 370,000 inventory records from 37 stores of one retailer, 65% did not](https://ideas.repec.org/a/inm/ormnsc/v54y2008i4p627-641.html).

## What the system assumed

[Store 014's book stock is the last count plus deliveries, minus sales and write-offs](../retail/technical/#tests-that-pass). [The store counts everything at the start and end of the month](../demo/#world-title), and in between, every report treats the book as right.

## What happened

[At the month-end count, 8 of the store's 11 item records were wrong](../demo/#world-title), by anything from one unit to 55. Across June, [the book disagreed with 21 of the store's 25 stock counts](../who-saw-what/#join-the-records-two-at-a-time), most by a few units.

Two of the gaps were large. [A shelf check at 11:00 on 12 June found the 5 kg atta bay empty with 55 bags on the books](../who-saw-what/#join-the-records-two-at-a-time), and [a count on 26 June found 63 bags of 5 kg atta that were not there](../who-saw-what/#join-the-records-two-at-a-time).

## Why the number missed it

The book is computed, not observed. [It agrees with every receipt and every sale, which is why the store's reconciliation test passes for every item on every day](../retail/technical/#tests-that-pass).

What it cannot see is everything that happens outside a transaction: [bags put away in the wrong bay](../retail/#what-actually-happened), [a 1 kg bag scanned at the till as 5 kg](../retail/technical/#finding-the-censored-hours), or [a pallet that held the wrong pack under the right label](../logistics/#what-actually-happened).

## How to catch it

Test the book against observations between counts. [A shelf check that finds the bay empty while the books show stock fails one of the store's domain tests](../retail/technical/#tests-that-ask-about-the-world), and so does [an item that goes far longer without a sale than its usual rate allows](../retail/technical/#finding-the-censored-hours).

Then count when a test fails, not only when the calendar says so. [In the study of 37 stores, stores with stronger auditing practices had more accurate records](https://ideas.repec.org/a/inm/ormnsc/v54y2008i4p627-641.html).

## If an AI agent reads it

Asked whether 5 kg atta was in stock on Thursday 11 June, [the series' scripted AI assistant, given only the schema, said yes, with 55 bags on hand](../retail/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer); [the shelf was empty all day](../retail/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer). Given the till lines and the shelf audits, [the same assistant answered that the bags were on the books but not on the shelf](../retail/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer).

## Try it on your own data

Pick twenty items and count them this week, without warning. Note how many records were right and how far off the others were. The system has been assuming that number is 100%.
