[The Store 014 Bench](../kit/#bench) asks an AI assistant 22 questions about June at Store 014: [seven on the shelf, seven on the truck, seven on the money, and one across them](../kit/#bench). [Each question is asked at a stated time, has an answer in a fixed form, and is scored against the simulation's answer key](../kit/bench/README.md), so an assistant's answers are counted, not judged.

## How the series' assistant scored

[The series' assistant is scripted: it runs fixed queries for each question, so its runs repeat exactly](../kit/#bench).

[Given only the schema, it gets 3 of 22 right](../kit/#bench). [Given the three domain layers from the stories, it gets 21](../kit/#bench). [Given the map of who saw what as well, it gets all 22](../kit/#bench).

The one question the domain layers miss is the one that crosses departments: [at 20:00 on Tuesday 23 June, why has 5 kg atta stopped selling, and which shipment and line are to blame?](../kit/bench/questions.json) [Answering it takes the trace across the store and the warehouse](../who-saw-what/#across-the-shelf-and-the-truck).

## What the questions look like

Each question names its answer's form, so a scorer can check it. Three of them:

- [What was 5 kg atta's on-shelf availability, 1 to 14 June?](../kit/bench/questions.json) Answer with a share between 0 and 1. [Given the schema, the assistant said 100%; the answer key says 78%](../retail/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer).
- [Which delivery should the store raise a claim against?](../kit/bench/questions.json) Answer with a shipment, a pallet and a line. [Given the schema, the assistant said none](../logistics/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer).
- [How long did customers who paid twice wait for their money?](../kit/bench/questions.json) Answer with the median days from the debit, and how many were never refunded. [Given the schema, the assistant measured from the complaint](../payments/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer).

## Run it on your assistant

1. Download [the bench](../kit/store-014-bench.zip), with its questions, answer key, scorer and baselines, and [the records](../data/).
2. Give your assistant the records it would have in your setting. Ask each question with only the rows timestamped before its time.
3. Write the answers as one JSON object keyed by question id, in the form [questions.json](../kit/bench/questions.json) gives.
4. Run `python score.py answers.json`. [The scorer needs Python 3 and nothing else](../kit/bench/README.md).

Run it twice: once with your schema alone, and once with a domain layer, such as [Store 014's, as YAML](../kit/store-014-domain-layer.yaml). The difference between the two scores is what the domain layer is worth to your assistant.
