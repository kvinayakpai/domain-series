On-time arrival measures whether a delivery reaches the dock inside its agreed window. Retailers enforce the window both ways: [Walmart began fining suppliers for early as well as late deliveries in 2017](https://www.mhlnews.com/global-supply-chain/article/22054435/walmart-suppliers-will-be-fined-for-both-early-and-late-deliveries), and [warehouses benchmark on-time shipment too, with best in class at 99.5% or better](https://www.yale.com/globalassets/coms/yale/north-america/documents/white-papers/snack-drawer/YALE-1520_WERC-DC-Metrics-infographic.pdf).

## What the scorecard said

[Store 014's delivery slot is 06:00 to 06:30](../logistics/#monday-612-in-the-morning), and [the warehouse truck made it on 23 of June's 25 mornings](../logistics/#five-readings-of-one-delivery). On 22 June, [it backed into the bay at 06:12](../logistics/#monday-612-in-the-morning).

## What happened

[That truck carried 16 bales of 1 kg atta on a line the notice called 5 kg](../logistics/#what-actually-happened). [The 5 kg shelf ran out the next afternoon and stayed empty for 51.5 open hours](../logistics/#what-actually-happened). The delivery was on time, and the shelf was empty anyway.

## Why the number missed it

Arrival time is a fact about the truck, not about what it carried: [the shipments table comes from the trip log, which cannot know what the truck carried](../logistics/technical/#the-records).

## How to catch it

Judge on-time together with what was on the pallet, and measure when the stock is on the shelf, not only when the truck arrives.

Expect the two to pull against each other. [Had the warehouse weighed every pallet, it would have found the light one, re-picked the line, and sent the truck at 05:00, reaching the store at 06:42, outside the slot](../logistics/#what-would-have-helped). [The scorecard would have fallen to 84%, and only one shopper all week would have missed a 5 kg bag](../logistics/#what-would-have-helped).

## If an AI agent reads it

Asked how many deliveries arrived inside the slot, [the series' scripted AI assistant answered 23 of 25, correctly, from the schema alone](../logistics/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer). [It was the only one of its seven questions it got right that way](../logistics/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer): arrival time is the part of a delivery the records see well. An agent that ranks a warehouse on it alone would reward the warehouse for not checking.
