Is 99.6% a good fill rate? That depends on what is being counted. [Fill rate is the share of demand met from stock](https://www.lokad.com/fill-rate-definition/), and between a warehouse and a store, [it can be counted by order, by line or by case](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/defining-on-time-in-full-in-the-consumer-sector).

## What the report said

By the shipping notices, [Store 014's June orders were 99.6% filled: 686 of the 689 cases ordered went out](../logistics/technical/#fill-rate-by-the-notice-and-by-the-item). [Two lines were short, by a case of dal and two of sugar](../logistics/technical/#fill-rate-by-the-notice-and-by-the-item).

## What happened

On 22 June, [16 cases on the 5 kg atta line held 1 kg packs](../logistics/technical/#fill-rate-by-the-notice-and-by-the-item). That is the right number of cases of the wrong item. [The picker had scanned the 5 kg slot's label and taken the bales from the slot next to it](../logistics/#what-actually-happened).

Counting only cases of the right item, [June's fill rate was 97.2%](../logistics/technical/#fill-rate-by-the-notice-and-by-the-item).

**99.6% by the notice, and 97.2% by the item.**

## Why the number missed it

[The shipping notice is written from the pick confirmations, and a pick is confirmed by scanning the slot's label](../logistics/technical/#the-records). So the notice knows how many cases left, and not what was in them.

Real receivers rarely check either. In [Auburn University's 2017 study of shipping notices](https://www.scdigest.com/assets/reps/2018_ASN_Study.pdf), retail warehouses physically checked an average of 57% of orders, and the median accuracy those checks found was 92%, against a goal of 98%.

## How to catch it

Check the item, and not just the count. [Scanning the barcode on each case instead of the slot's label rejects a 1 kg bale on a 5 kg line](../logistics/#what-would-have-helped).

Or weigh the pallet. [With 1 kg bales on the 5 kg line, the first pallet weighed about 564 kg against the 724 kg its notice implied, 22% light](../logistics/#what-would-have-helped).
