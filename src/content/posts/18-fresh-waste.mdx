Fresh food forces a trade-off: order too much and it expires, too little and shoppers leave without it, the problem operations researchers call [the newsvendor model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsvendor_model). The pressure to cut waste is real: [of the 1.05 billion tonnes of food the world wasted in 2022, the UN puts 12% in shops](https://unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/world-squanders-over-1-billion-meals-day-un-report).

## What the records said

[Store 014's pouch milk arrives at 05:30 on a standing order and is good for 48 hours](../retail/technical/#same-store-different-item-milk), in line with [pouch milk sold in India](https://amul.com/products/amul-taaza-info.php). The records show what sold and what expired, and on those two numbers [the order that earns the most is 5% below today's](../retail/technical/#same-store-different-item-milk).

With it, [the margin on toned milk after waste rises from ₹4,121 to ₹10,810 in June](../retail/technical/#same-store-different-item-milk). Every record says the change works.

## What happened

Because the store is simulated, we can see the other side: with the leaner order, [shoppers leave without 269 pouches of toned milk in June instead of 119](../retail/technical/#same-store-different-item-milk), and [the milk sells out on 6 days instead of 2](../retail/technical/#same-store-different-item-milk).

Whether those shoppers bought the rest of their basket somewhere else, no record will say.

## Why the number missed it

[A till records milk sales up to the moment the crate empties, and nothing after](../retail/technical/#same-store-different-item-milk). Demand above the sell-out is never recorded, so in the records cutting the order looks free.

## How to catch it

Record the time of each day's last sale. A crate that empties at six in the evening means the shelf stood empty until closing, and [the item's usual evening sales give an estimate of what was missed](../retail/technical/#same-store-different-item-milk). Then put waste and estimated lost sales in the same table, and choose the order on both.

## If an AI agent reads it

An agent told to cut waste will find the leaner order, and [the margin after waste will tell it that it was right](../retail/technical/#same-store-different-item-milk). Only an estimate of the shoppers who left without milk can tell it otherwise.

## Try it on your own data
