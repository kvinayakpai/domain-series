Which table in your system has a column called "charged twice"? I have not found one yet. A customer is charged twice when a payment goes through but looks failed, and they pay again. For payments that fail outright, [RBI requires a customer's money back within five days when a merchant never confirms the payment, with ₹100 a day in compensation after that](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=11693). A double charge on a bill that was paid again falls outside that rule, so [refunding it is up to the store](../payments/technical/#clocks-that-start-at-the-harm).

## What the records said

I asked the series' scripted AI assistant at the month-end review whether any customer paid twice in June.

Me – Did any customer pay twice in June?

Assistant (given the till's schema) – [No, all 30,077 bills have exactly one approved payment](../payments/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer)

[All ten data tests on the payment records passed](../payments/technical/#tests-that-pass).

## What happened

[In June, 24 customers paid twice: 26 extra charges, ₹13,366.06](../payments/#what-actually-happened). [15 of them paid during 90 minutes on Saturday 20 June](../payments/#what-actually-happened), when the bank's UPI switch slowed down, and the rest on ordinary days.

Take [the customer at 6:48 that Saturday evening](../payments/#saturday-648-in-the-evening). [Her bank took ₹1,064.47, the till showed "failed: no response", and she paid again by card](../payments/#saturday-648-in-the-evening). [87 seconds after she approved the first payment, the store's bank confirmed it](../payments/#saturday-648-in-the-evening).

**Zero double charges in the records, and 24 customers in reality.**

## Why the number missed it

No record says "charged twice". [A till that hears nothing back never learns the payment's reference number, so the provider's success has nothing to join to](../payments/#why-the-data-could-not-say-so). [The only record of the double charge was in the customer's bank statement](../payments/#why-the-data-could-not-say-so).

## How to catch it

Match timeouts to the provider's report by amount and time, then look for bills that were paid again. [The query finds all 26 extra charges, with no false alarms](../payments/technical/#the-extra-charges).

[Run each morning on the provider's file, it finds them a median 0.6 days after the debit, against 2 days for complaints, and three customers never complained at all](../payments/#what-domain-knowledge-changes-in-the-data-work).

Some can be prevented. [Had the till waited for the status check NPCI allows at 90 seconds before asking for another payment, double charges in June would have fallen from 24 customers to 9](../payments/#what-would-have-helped).

## If an AI agent reads it

An agent that reads only the till will report zero. Given the provider's report, the same assistant answers differently.

Assistant (given the provider's report) – [Yes, 24 customers, 26 extra charges, ₹13,366.06](../payments/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer)
