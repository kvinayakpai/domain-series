When a till says "payment failed", does that mean the customer did not pay? Payment success rate is the share of payment attempts that succeed, as the till or the payment gateway sees them. At an Indian supermarket most attempts are [UPI, India's instant payment system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_Payments_Interface), which [processed 24.07 billion transactions in September 2026](https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/upi-processes-2407-billion-transactions-worth-rs-2937-lakh-crore-in-september).

## What the dashboard said

[Across June, Store 014's tills approved 95.9% of UPI attempts](../payments/#five-readings-of-one-payment). On Saturday 20 June, [approvals fell to 69% and 67% in the 18:00 and 19:00 hours](../payments/#five-readings-of-one-payment), and between 18:20 and 19:50 [the tills approved 61% of UPI attempts](../payments/technical/#rates-by-the-hour).

[The incident was closed as a slow switch at the store's bank](../payments/#five-readings-of-one-payment), and [cashiers had asked for another payment 30 times](../payments/#five-readings-of-one-payment).

## What happened

Many of those "failed" payments had already left the customer's account. [Between 18:20 and 19:50, 82% of UPI attempts ended as a credit at the store's bank](../payments/technical/#rates-by-the-hour).

The difference was customers whose payment the till gave up on, and who then paid again. [15 customers paid twice in those 90 minutes, two of them three times](../payments/#what-actually-happened).

**The till approved 61%, and the bank received 82%.**

## Why the number missed it

"Failed" at the till meant no answer within 30 seconds. [When the answer is late, UPI settles the payment afterwards: the merchant's bank either confirms the credit or the money goes back](../payments/#what-actually-happened), as [NPCI's 2017 circular on debit reversals and deemed approval](https://www.npci.org.in/PDF/npci/upi/circular/2017/Circular-17-Debit-Reversals-and-Deemed-Approval_0.pdf) sets out.

The till never finds out. [A till that hears nothing back never learns the payment's reference number, so there is nothing to join the late answer to](../payments/#why-the-data-could-not-say-so). Slow answers are not rare either. [On 12 April 2025, UPI's success rate fell to about 50% for nearly two hours](https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/upi-outage-attributed-to-surge-in-api-calls-from-banks-says-npci).

## How to catch it

Match each timeout to the provider's final status. [All 48 of June's timeouts match a row in the provider's report by amount and time, within a second](../payments/technical/#the-key-that-is-not-there): 26 were credited late, 6 were debited and returned, and 16 never left the customer's account.

## If an AI agent reads it

Let me summarize how the series' scripted assistant answered when I asked about that evening.

Me – What happened to the 28 payments the tills gave up on?

Assistant (given the till's schema) – [They failed and the customers paid another way](../payments/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer)

Assistant (given the provider's report) – [17 had been credited late, so those customers paid twice](../payments/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer)

An agent at the till that asks for another payment whenever the till says "failed" would do what the cashiers did, 30 times in 90 minutes.
