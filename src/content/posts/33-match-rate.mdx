What happens to a payment that nobody can match? In most systems it goes to a pile with a polite name. Reconciliation pairs the money a payment provider reports with the sales a store recorded, and the match rate is the share that pairs up automatically. For UPI the pairing key is [the payment's reference number, the RRN](../payments/#why-the-data-could-not-say-so).

## What the report said

[On Saturday 20 June there were 607 UPI credits against 597 approved payments, and on Sunday 670 against 663](../payments/#five-readings-of-one-payment). [The 17 credits with no approved payment behind them went into a suspense account until someone claimed them](../payments/#five-readings-of-one-payment).

## What happened

Every one was a customer's money. [Each of the 17 matches, by amount and time, a payment the till gave up on, on a bill the customer then paid again](../payments/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer). [Together they were ₹7,825.69, owed to 15 customers](../payments/#what-actually-happened).

**The unmatched pile was not noise, it was 15 customers.**

## Why the number missed it

[A till that gives up on a payment never learns its reference number](../payments/#why-the-data-could-not-say-so), so a match by reference cannot work. The key is missing, and not wrong. The credits that cannot be matched are exactly the ones that need a person.

And the till cannot simply keep asking. [NPCI's rules put the first status check on a payment no sooner than 90 seconds after it, and preferably no more than three within two hours](https://www.medianama.com/2025/04/223-npci-new-guidelines-upi-outage/).

## How to catch it

Match on what both sides do have. [Pairing each timeout with a provider row of the same amount that started within five seconds, nearest first, matches all 48 of June's timeouts](../payments/technical/#the-key-that-is-not-there). [The window matters, because customers who retried by UPI made a second attempt for the same amount 30 seconds later](../payments/technical/#the-key-that-is-not-there).

Better still, give every request a key. [With the bill ID on every UPI request, the next morning's match finds every extra charge, the median wait for a refund falls from 4.9 days to 0.7, and complaints fall from 26 to 12](../payments/#what-would-have-helped).

## If an AI agent reads it

Assistant (given the provider's report) – [They are extra charges, owed to 15 customers](../payments/technical/#the-assistant-with-and-without-a-domain-layer)

## Try it on your own data

Age every item in your suspense account. For each one older than a day, look for a failed or timed-out payment of the same amount within a minute of it.

So in summary, the credits that fail to match are the ones where the customers are.
