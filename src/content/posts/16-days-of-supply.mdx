How many days will your stock last? Days of supply turns the stock on hand into time, the number of days it will last at the expected rate of sales. Finance teams use the same idea as [days inventory outstanding](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/days-inventory-outstanding/), the average number of days a company holds its stock before selling it.

## What the system said

During the gap in mid-June, [Store 014's books showed 56 bags of 5 kg atta](../retail/#what-actually-happened). At [the store's own forecast of about 24 bags a day](../retail/#five-readings-of-one-week), that is 2.3 days of supply.

[With 56 bags above the reorder point of 40, the store ordered nothing](../retail/#what-actually-happened).

## What happened

[The shelf had been empty since 15:48 on Wednesday 10 June, and the 56 bags were behind the rice](../retail/#what-actually-happened). The 2.3 days were days of nothing on the shelf.

It happened again after the wrong pallet. [For three nights the reorder system saw 63 bags of 5 kg on the books against a reorder point of 40 or 41, and ordered nothing](../logistics/#what-actually-happened). [Those bags had never left the warehouse](../logistics/#monday-612-in-the-morning).

**2.3 days of supply, and none of it on the shelf.**

## Why the number missed it

Both halves of the ratio were wrong, and both errors make the number look longer. The stock was on the books but not on the shelf, because [book stock is computed from transactions and never looks at the shelf](../retail/technical/#tests-that-pass). And the forecast was too low, because [it learnt from sales that the empty shelf had already cut](../retail/technical/#unconstraining-demand).

Real chains have lost much more this way. At Target Canada, [errors in item data left stores short while the distribution centers overflowed](https://mprnews.org/story/2016/01/29/target-canada-failure), and the chain closed within two years of opening.

## How to catch it

Recompute days of supply on what someone has seen: stock confirmed by a shelf check or a count, and [demand estimated with the empty hours filled in](../retail/technical/#unconstraining-demand). For an item that has gone quiet, treat its days of supply as unknown until someone looks.

[The store's gap detector flagged 5 kg atta three open hours after the shelf emptied](../retail/technical/#finding-the-censored-hours), with all 56 bags still on the books.

## If an AI agent reads it

Now I needn't tell you who acts on this number. [The store's reorder rule already acts on book stock automatically](../demo/#world-title), with no one checking the shelf in between. [A scripted agent given the rule "in stock means on the shelf" asked for a shelf check on the first night](../retail/#now-let-an-agent-act-on-it), and the shelf was empty for 7 open hours instead of 39.
