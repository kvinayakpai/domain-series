Every time a store cuts its milk order, the waste report gets better. Does anyone ask what the shoppers did?

## What is it?

Fresh waste is the stock that expires before it sells, usually reported against sales or margin. The dairy or fresh manager uses it to set standing orders for milk, bread and produce. The pressure to cut it is real. As per the UN, [of the 1.05 billion tonnes of food the world wasted in 2022, 12% was in shops](https://unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/world-squanders-over-1-billion-meals-day-un-report).

Behind it sits a trade-off that operations researchers call [the newsvendor model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsvendor_model). Order too much and it expires, too little and shoppers leave without it.

## What did it say at Store 014?

Store 014 is a supermarket in Jayanagar, Bengaluru, that exists only in a simulation I built. I followed eleven everyday items through June 2026, hour by hour.

Its pouch milk arrives at 05:30 on a standing order and is good for 48 hours, like [pouch milk sold in India](https://amul.com/products/amul-taaza-info.php). On the records of what sold and what expired, the best order for toned milk is 5% below today's. With it, June's margin on toned milk after waste rises from ₹4,121 to ₹10,810, and every record says the change works.

With the leaner order, shoppers leave without 269 pouches of toned milk in June instead of 119, and the milk sells out on 6 days instead of 2.

**The margin after waste more than doubled, and so did the shoppers who left without milk.**

## What could it not see?

A till records milk sales until the crate empties, and nothing after. Demand above the sell-out is never recorded, so in the records cutting the order always looks free.

Whether those shoppers bought the rest of their basket somewhere else, no record will say.

## How do you catch it?

- Record the time of each day's last sale. A crate that empties at six in the evening means an empty shelf until closing, and the item's usual evening sales estimate what was missed.
- Put waste and estimated lost sales in the same table, and choose the order on both.

So in summary, for fresh food the waste report shows one side of the trade-off, and someone has to put the other side next to it. The rule I would give any AI agent that reads it – **Waste and empty shelves trade off. Never optimize one without estimating the other.**
