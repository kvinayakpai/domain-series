STORE 014 ONTOLOGY. Season 2's hand-built ontology of this store. Follow it over table and column names.

Its things: product, item, place, customer, store order, pick, case, pallet, shipment, receiving, stock, stock change, forecast, demand, sale, lost sale, bill, payment attempt, debit, settlement, refund, complaint. Demand (a customer wants an item) and lost sales (demand that found the shelf empty) are in no table.

What each table is:
- store.pos_lines: Till lines: every sale as scanned (sku_scanned, qty, bill_ts). Looks at the world. Sales, not demand.
- store.daily_sales: The till lines totalled per item per day. Sales, not demand.
- store.inventory_book: Book stock: what the system believes is on hand at close. It copies other records (the last count, plus receipts, minus sales and write-offs) and never looks at the shelf.
- store.shelf_audits: Staff looking at the shelf at 11:00, twice a week: status 'on shelf' or 'empty'. The only record of what was on the shelf.
- store.stock_counts: Physical counts of the stock, and the corrections they made to the book. Looks at the world.
- store.goods_receipts: Pallets received from the warehouse, as booked. Deliveries into the store, not sales. Copies the shipping notice.
- store.forecasts: The nightly forecast of demand, and the order it made, for each warehouse item. Not sales.
- store.write_offs: Stock thrown away or written off, with a reason.
- store.store_orders: Orders the store sent to the warehouse.
- store.fresh_deliveries: Milk, curd and bread delivered each morning by the dairy and bakery routes.
- warehouse.pick_confirmations: The warehouse's pick confirmations for the store's orders.
- warehouse.shipping_notice_lines: What each shipping notice said was on each pallet. Copies the picks.
- warehouse.pallets: One row per pallet label (sscc). Copies the shipping notice.
- warehouse.shipments: Trucks: dispatch, delivery slot and arrival.
- warehouse.stock_adjustments: Corrections the warehouse made to its stock of the store's lines.
- payments.pos_payments: The till's view of each payment attempt: till_status, and the rrn when the till got one.
- payments.psp_upi_report: UPI's report: each UPI payment's final status and when it was known. Looks at the world.
- payments.upi_settlements: The acquirer's daily UPI settlement. Copies UPI's report.
- payments.upi_refunds: Refunds the store made for UPI payments.
- payments.customer_complaints: Customer complaints about payments.

Joins:
- every sku column (sku, sku_scanned, asn_sku, sku_booked) = store.products.sku
- store.pos_lines.bill_id = payments.pos_payments.bill_id
- payments.pos_payments.rrn = payments.psp_upi_report.rrn
- payments.psp_upi_report.bill_ref = payments.pos_payments.bill_id, when bill_ref is set
- payments.upi_refunds.rrn = payments.psp_upi_report.rrn
- store.goods_receipts.sscc = warehouse.pallets.sscc
- store.goods_receipts.shipment_id = warehouse.shipments.shipment_id
- warehouse.shipping_notice_lines.sscc = warehouse.pallets.sscc
- warehouse.pick_confirmations.order_id = store.store_orders.order_id

Rules, each with what it means here:
1. Available means on the shelf. Book stock is a belief – the last count, plus receipts, minus sales and write-offs.
   Here: On-shelf availability is the share of store.shelf_audits rows with status 'on shelf'. Never compute it from store.inventory_book.
2. Silence is a question. No sales while the books show stock means someone should look at the shelf.
   Here: Days with no sales in store.pos_lines while store.inventory_book shows stock mean the shelf may be empty: check store.shelf_audits.
3. Demand is not sales. Demand that finds an empty shelf becomes a lost sale, which no record holds – estimate it and say how.
   Here: Units sold come from store.pos_lines (or store.daily_sales). If asked how many shoppers left without an item, or for lost sales, do not count goods receipts, shelf audits, forecasts or write-offs as shoppers or sales. Say that no record holds them, and if you estimate them, say how. Give what the records show: the shelf audits that found the item missing, and its sales by day.
4. A forecast predicts demand, so score it against demand, not sales.
   Here: store.forecasts predict demand. Do not score them against store.daily_sales alone.
5. Every stock correction has a reason. Mirror-image corrections on lookalike items, neighboring slots or two buildings are one event.
   Here: store.stock_counts.adjustment and warehouse.stock_adjustments need a reason. Mirror-image corrections on lookalike items or across the two buildings are one event.
6. Fresh stock expires. Waste and the empty shelf are one decision – count both.
   Here: For fresh items, store.write_offs and the shelf audits that found the shelf empty are one decision.
7. An item is the pack, known by the code on the pack. Match on that code – never on the slot, the pallet label or the product name.
   Here: Match items on store.products.gtin or sku (pos_lines.gtin_scanned, shipping_notice_lines.gtin), never on a slot, an sscc or the product name.
8. A delivery is on time, in full and right – checked case by case at the door, and done only when the stock is on the shelf.
   Here: A delivery is done only when the stock is on the shelf: compare warehouse.shipments.arrived_ts, store.goods_receipts and later shelf audits.
9. A payment has three witnesses – the till, UPI and the banks. A timeout at the till means “not known yet”, and UPI's final status decides.
   Here: A payments.pos_payments.till_status of 'failed: no response' is a timeout: not known yet. payments.psp_upi_report.status ('success', 'failed', 'returned') decides.
10. One bill, one payment. Every UPI request carries the bill ID, and a debit with no approved payment is a customer charged twice until proven otherwise.
   Here: A UPI 'success' in payments.psp_upi_report with no approved payment for its bill at the till is a customer charged twice until proven otherwise.
11. A refund answers a debit. The clock starts at the extra debit, not at the complaint.
   Here: Refund time runs from the extra debit (payments.psp_upi_report.status_ts) to payments.upi_refunds.completed_ts, not from the complaint.
12. Every record either looks at the world or copies another record. Before acting on a KPI, find the record it reads and an observation that can contradict it.
   Here: Before trusting a number, name the table it reads and a table that could contradict it, such as store.shelf_audits against store.inventory_book.
