Draw the records as a map: what writes each one, what it looks at, and which KPI reads it. The KPIs read copies: OTIF reads the goods receipts, on-shelf availability reads the book stock, payment success reads the till payments. The records that look at the world, counts, audits and the provider's report, are read by no headline KPI. And one thing had no record looking at it at all: what was on a pallet. None of June's 30 pallets was weighed or case-scanned, all 70 picks were confirmed by scanning the slot, and all 70 receipts by scanning the label. Most of the map can be drawn from the schema; the edges to the world are what only domain knowledge can draw.
