The series’ assistant and agent are scripted so every run can be repeated. Sequeda, Allemang and Jacob, arXiv:2311.07509.

Agents act on records, so an agent's mistakes are its records' mistakes, made at the speed of software. Across the three posts, the same scripted assistant answered 3 of 21 questions right given the schema, and all 21 given a domain layer. A scripted replenishment agent lost 214 bags of 5 kg atta in an average June reading the KPIs as reported, and 105 reading them with the domain rules. Outside this synthetic world, the insurance benchmark that dbt Labs reran in 2026 was built by data.world in 2023 to test this: GPT-4 answered 16 percent of its questions from the SQL tables and 54 percent from a knowledge graph of the business.
