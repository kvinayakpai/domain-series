The series’ assistant and agent are scripted so every run can be repeated. dbt Labs (7 April 2026), Semantic Layer vs. Text-to-SQL: 2026 benchmark.

Agents act on records, so an agent's mistakes are its records' mistakes, made at the speed of software. Across the three posts, the same scripted assistant answered 3 of 21 questions right given the schema, and all 21 given a domain layer. A scripted replenishment agent lost 214 bags of 5 kg atta in an average June reading the KPIs as reported, and 105 reading them with the domain rules. Outside this synthetic world, in dbt Labs’ 2026 benchmark, current AI models answered about 65 percent of 11 business questions about an insurance database correctly from the raw tables, and 98 to 100 percent through a semantic layer once three data models had spelled out the business’s terms. Text-to-SQL on the same models reached 84 to 90 percent, and dbt Labs sells the semantic layer, so treat it as direction, not proof.
