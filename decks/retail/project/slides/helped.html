The demo lets you try two changes the store could make from the first of June, on exactly the same customers. A low-sales alert flags an item selling under a quarter of its forecast while the books show stock and sends someone to count it the next morning: unmet demand falls from 157 bags to 90. Scanning every case into a numbered backroom bin, with fixed refill rounds, prevents the misplacement altogether and, as a bonus, catches the wrong pallet on 22 June at put-away: unmet demand falls to a single bag. Telling which kind of fix a failure needs is what domain knowledge is for.
