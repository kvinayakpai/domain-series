This is the first post in a series on why domain knowledge matters. Every number in this deck comes from a synthetic world: one fictional supermarket in Jayanagar, Bengaluru, its warehouse and its milk route, simulated hour by hour through June 2026 and calibrated against published studies of real stores. The headline is the whole story in two lines: the system said 55 bags of atta were on hand, and the shelf had been empty since Wednesday.
