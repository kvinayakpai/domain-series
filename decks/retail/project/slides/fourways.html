On-shelf availability of 5 kg atta at Store 014 in June. None of these is wrong. A dashboard that shows one without saying which one it is, is.

Ask how available 5 kg atta was in June and you can get four honest answers. The system counts hours with stock on the books, so the cases behind the rice count as available: 97 percent. The shelf audit visits twice a week at 11 in the morning and happened to catch the empty bay on two of eight visits: 75 percent. Counting open hours with a bag on the shelf gives 77 percent, and weighting each hour by how many customers wanted a bag gives 78. Same shelf, same month, four numbers. The question to ask of any availability figure is which one it is.
