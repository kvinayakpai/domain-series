Each reads its inputs correctly. None of them can see the shelf.

Nobody here is careless; each data product reads its inputs correctly. The pipeline runs twelve data tests every night, keys, values, reconciliations, and the book against its own ledger. Eleven pass, and the one failure is full-cream milk below zero, a familiar scan-error symptom. The dashboard computes on-shelf availability from book stock: 97%. The forecast, a 28-day mean of recorded sales, eases from 24.3 to 20.2 bags a day, and scored against recorded sales it looks 13% too high, so the obvious tuning lowers it. The mix analysis sees five kilo’s share of atta fall from 40% to 25%. And the AI assistant, asked seven questions about the first half of June, gets one right: the one about what the tills scanned.
