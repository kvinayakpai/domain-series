Five readings of one shelf

WhoWhat they seeWhat they conclude
Store managerAn empty bay, and a handheld that says 55“It must be on the next truck.”
Reorder system55 bags on the books, reorder point 40“In stock. No reorder needed.”
Demand plannerSales down 59% in a week; forecast 24.3 → 20.2 a day“Demand is falling. Trim the order.”
Category manager5 kg share of atta units: 40% → 25%“Shoppers are moving to 1 kg.”
Supplier’s sales repStore orders: 172 → 112 bags a week“A competitor is taking the space.”

Each conclusion is reasonable given the record in front of that person. Each is wrong.

Nobody in this story is careless. Four people and one system each read the record in front of them. The store manager sees the empty bay and a handheld that contradicts it. The reorder system sees 55 bags against a reorder point of 40 and orders nothing, three nights running. The demand planner sees 217 bags sold in the first week of June and 88 in the second, and a forecast drifting from 24.3 to 20.2 bags a day. The category manager sees 5 kg’s share of atta fall from 40% to 25%. The supplier’s rep sees orders from the store drop from 172 bags to 112. Every conclusion follows from its record. Every one is wrong.
