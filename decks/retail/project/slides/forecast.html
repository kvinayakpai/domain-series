Recorded sales are capped by what was on the shelf. The planner sees a volatile item; the truth is a steady item with a broken shelf.

The forecast has the same problem in a quieter form. It is a 28-day average of recorded sales, and recorded sales are capped by what was on the shelf. Before the gap it read 24.3 bags a day; by the end of June it had slid to 20.2. Measured against recorded sales, it was off by 54 percent; against what customers actually wanted, by 33. Filling the empty-shelf hours with expected sales, using only data the store has, would have kept it at 25.7.
