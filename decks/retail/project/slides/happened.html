Here is what nobody could see. On Tuesday 9 June at 06:17 the warehouse truck delivered 14 cases of 5 kg atta. The receiving clerk scanned the pallet label, the books went up by 56 bags, and the cases were put away behind the rice stack, in the wrong bay. The shelf sold through what it had and ran out at 3:48 on Wednesday afternoon. From then on, every bag the system counted was behind the rice. The reorder run saw 55 or 56 bags each night, above the reorder point, and ordered nothing. Staff restacking the rice found the cases at ten on Saturday. That is 39 open hours with an empty shelf.
