Here is what the answer key holds. On Tuesday the ninth at 06:17, fourteen cases of five kilo atta from the morning truck were put away behind the rice stack, in the wrong bay. On Wednesday at 15:48 the shelf ran out. The books said 56 bags, above the reorder point of 40, so nothing was ordered, and the shelf stood empty for 39 open hours while the system showed stock, until staff restacking the rice found the cases on Saturday morning. Shoppers asked for 71 bags they could not find. Demand did ease that week, after the first week of the month when households stock up: 224 bags wanted in the first week, 148 in the second. That explains part of the drop in sales. The rest was an empty shelf.
