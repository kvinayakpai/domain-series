Milk breaks the pattern, which is the point. It never passes through the warehouse: the dairy drops a standing order at 5:30 in the morning, and pouch milk is good for 48 hours from packing. There is no reorder point to freeze. The risk runs the other way. At today’s order, 291 pouches expired in June, eating 7,188 rupees of the 11,309 the store made on milk. Cut the order by 5 percent and what milk earns more than doubles, while the pouches customers wanted and could not find rise from 119 to 269. Which is right depends on how many of those shoppers came in for milk and would have bought a whole basket.
