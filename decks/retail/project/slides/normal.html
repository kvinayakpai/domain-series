It would be comforting to call this a freak event. The research says it is normal. A worldwide study of grocery out-of-stocks for FMI, GMA and CIES put the average out-of-stock rate at 8.3 percent and traced about 47 percent to store ordering and forecasting and 25 percent to shelf management: most empty shelves start inside the store. DeHoratius and Raman examined nearly 370,000 inventory records from 37 stores of one retailer and found 65 percent inaccurate. The synthetic world is calibrated to those figures.
