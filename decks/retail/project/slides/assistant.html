Why did 5 kg atta sales fall? With the domain layer:

From Wed 10 Jun, 15:53 to Sat 13 Jun, 10:43 the tills recorded one bag in 39 open hours, against 68 expected, while the books showed 55–56 bags. The shelf audit at Fri 12 Jun, 11:00 found the bay empty. No delivery arrived before sales resumed, so the bags were in the store but not on the shelf: misplaced or not refilled. With those hours filled in, the week comes to about 155 bags against 217: the empty shelf explains about half of the fall, and the rest is demand easing after a strong first week. Nothing says shoppers changed packs.
