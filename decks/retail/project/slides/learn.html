How to learn this domain

How stores run

Raman, DeHoratius and Ton, Execution: The Missing Link in Retail Operations (2001). Then walk a supermarket and note where stock lives.

Inventory policy

Reorder points, min/max, safety stock, service levels. Then stock loss stalling replenishment: Yun Kang’s MIT thesis (2004).

Forecasting

Hyndman and Athanasopoulos, Forecasting: Principles and Practice (free online), then the M5 Walmart data on Kaggle.

Store data

Till lines, book stock, receipts, counts and audits, joined by GTIN, GLN and SSCC.

Practice

Download Store 014’s June records and try the four exercises before opening the answer key.

A rough plan for a few evenings a week over about two months. Links in the post: kvinayakpai.github.io/domain-series/retail

A rough plan for a few evenings a week over about two months. Start with how stores actually run: the California Management Review article by Raman, DeHoratius and Ton, then walk a supermarket and note where stock lives. Learn inventory policy: reorder points, min/max rules, safety stock and service levels, then read about stock loss stalling replenishment. Work through Hyndman and Athanasopoulos, free online, and try the M5 Walmart data. Learn the store’s tables and the identifiers that join them. Then practise on Store 014’s June records.
