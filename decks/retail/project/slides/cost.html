Customers wanted about 157 bags that week; the tills recorded 88. Of the 71 bags shoppers wanted during the gap and could not buy, 26 became sales of another brand, 13 a different pack size, and 19 became trips to another store. Eight shoppers came back later and five bags were never bought at all. Underneath, demand was flat: 24.7 bags a day in May, 24.9 in June. What fell was the shelf.
