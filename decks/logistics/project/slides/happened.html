Here is what nobody could see. At three in the morning the picker went to slot A-07-03 for 16 cases of 5 kg atta, scanned the slot’s label, and took 16 bales from A-07-04 next to it: the same brand’s 1 kg packs. The scan confirmed where the picker stood, not what the picker took. Every record after that was written from the pick confirmation: the shipping notice, the pallet label, whose SSCC points to the notice and says nothing about the contents, and the store’s goods receipt, booked by matching the label to the notice. They agree with each other perfectly, and with the pallet not at all.
