At the shelf price of ₹265 a bag. Shopper responses follow Gruen, Corsten and Bharadwaj (2002).

The 5 kg shelf was empty for 51.5 open hours. Customers wanted 72 bags they could not buy, ₹19,080 at the shelf price. 29 of those became trips to another store, 16 a different pack size and 12 another brand; 9 shoppers came back later and 6 bags were never bought.
