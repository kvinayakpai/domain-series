Sources: WERC DC Measures 2026 via Yale; Store 014, June 2026; GS1 UK, Logistics labels and SSCCs.

Mis-picks are a rate, not a freak event. WERC’s 2026 DC Measures benchmark puts best-in-class order-picking accuracy at 99.7 percent or better, which still means about one in three hundred goes wrong. Receiving against the shipping notice is the design: under GS1’s standards the shipper sends a despatch advice that identifies every pallet by its SSCC, so the receiver scans a label instead of counting. And nothing checks the notice against the goods: at Store 014 none of June’s 30 pallets was weighed or case-scanned, so nothing in the records could tell a pallet of 1 kg packs from one of 5 kg bags.
