OTIF per delivery for June, on the scorecard. Same customers and trucks in every scenario; the changes start on 1 June. Try them in the demo.

The demo lets you try three changes from the first of June, with the same customers and trucks. Scanning every case at pick prevents the mistake: the scanner rejects a 1 kg bale on a 5 kg line, and the scorecard and the shelf agree at 88%. Weighing every pallet at dispatch catches it: the first pallet is 160 kg light, the line is re-picked, and the truck reaches the store at 06:42, outside its slot, so the scorecard falls to 84% while the shelf stays full. Scanning every case at the store catches it on arrival: the clerk books what came and raises a claim, and the scorecard also falls to 84%, because it now records what arrived. A measure that only reads the sender’s records improves when you stop checking.
