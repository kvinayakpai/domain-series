OTIF for the warehouse’s 25 deliveries to Store 014 in June. The 22 June delivery passes the first three and fails the fourth, which nobody computes because nobody records it.

OTIF, on time and in full, sounds like one number. The warehouse measures its own dispatch: 92%. The scorecard measures arrival in the slot and fullness by the store’s goods receipt: 90% per order line, 88% per delivery. Only counting what was really on the pallets gives 84%. Real programmes move their definitions too: Walmart began fining suppliers three percent of the value of late or missing goods in 2017, early deliveries included; in 2019 full truckloads had to arrive within a two-day window 87 percent of the time; from September 2020 the target was 98. Before comparing two OTIF numbers, ask what window and which record each one uses.
