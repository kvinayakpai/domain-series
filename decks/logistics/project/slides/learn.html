How to learn this domain

How a warehouse works

Bartholdi and Hackman, Warehouse & Distribution Science (free from the authors), and Gwynne Richards, Warehouse Management. Then walk a warehouse, dock to dispatch.

Fundamentals

MIT’s Supply Chain Fundamentals (CTL.SC1x): demand, inventory and transport.

Documents and IDs

GS1 logistic label, SSCC and despatch advice. In India, the e-way bill for most consignments over ₹50,000 (rule 138, CGST Rules).

Metrics

OTIF, case fill, pick accuracy, dock-to-stock. How Walmart’s OTIF changed; WERC’s DC Measures.

Practice

Download June’s shipping records, compute OTIF two ways, and find the 22 June line.

A rough plan for a few evenings a week over about two months. Links in the post: kvinayakpai.github.io/domain-series/logistics

A rough plan for a few evenings a week over about two months. Read Bartholdi and Hackman’s Warehouse and Distribution Science, free from the authors, and Gwynne Richards’s Warehouse Management, then walk a warehouse from the receiving dock to dispatch. Take MIT’s Supply Chain Fundamentals. Learn the GS1 logistic label, the SSCC and the despatch advice, and in India the e-way bill. Learn the metrics and how their definitions differ. Then practise on Store 014’s June shipping records.
