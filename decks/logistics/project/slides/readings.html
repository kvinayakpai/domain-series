Each conclusion is reasonable given the records in front of it. Each misses the wrong line.

As in the retail story, nobody is careless, and each data product reads the records in front of it. The OTIF dashboard shows 88% for June, and counts 22 June as perfect. The data tests pass, all ten: the shipping notice, the pick confirmations, the pallets and the receipts join without a single mismatch. The inventory reports show four atta corrections in two buildings, netting to about ₹80. The store’s loss report, after a count on Friday 26 June, shows 63 bags of 5 kg gone and 157 packs of 1 kg from nowhere, and suspects sloppy counting or theft. And an AI assistant given the shipping schema says the 22 June delivery was on time and in full.
