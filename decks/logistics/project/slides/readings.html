Each conclusion is reasonable given the record in front of that person. Each misses the wrong line.

As in the retail story, nobody is careless. The DC shift supervisor sees pick confirmations for every line, and later two count variances in the atta bay, both corrected and closed. The transport manager sees a trip log: left at 04:30, arrived at 06:12, inside the slot, and on time on 23 of 25 mornings in June. The receiving clerk sees pallet labels that matched the notice. The OTIF scorecard shows 88% for June, and counts 22 June as perfect. And the stock controller, after a count on Friday 26 June, sees 63 bags of 5 kg gone and 157 packs of 1 kg from nowhere, and suspects sloppy counting or theft.
