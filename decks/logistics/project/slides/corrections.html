Equal and opposite variances in neighbouring slots, mirrored at a store that got a truck from them, is the signature of a mis-pick.

The mis-pick left four records wrong, two in each building, and each was fixed on its own. The warehouse found the 1 kg slot sixteen cases short on Tuesday and the 5 kg slot sixteen cases over on Thursday, and closed both as count variances. The store found its two in a single count on Friday, after complaints. Across the company they net to about ₹80, which is why nobody chases them. Read together, they are the signature of a mis-pick, and the pick confirmations would name the truck in a minute.
