Same records, same month. Grey: what the store reports. Red: the clock started at the debit, and the attempts counted the way UPI works.

Two of the store’s KPIs change when they are read the way the money moves. Refund wait, as support reports it, runs from the complaint: a median of 3 days. The customer lost the money at the extra debit, and from there the median is 4.9 days; when June closed, four extra charges, ₹2,846.06, were still unrefunded. RBI gives banks five days to return a failed payment to a customer; by that clock, 10 of the 22 refunds were late. And June’s technical decline rate, 0.76%, is in line with UPI as a whole, and it counts no timeouts at all. Read by the hour, with timeouts counted, the tills got no answer on 25% and 26% of attempts in the 18:00 and 19:00 hours on 20 June, the two hours in which 15 customers paid twice.
