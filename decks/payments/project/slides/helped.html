Same customers, baskets and banks in every scenario; the changes start on 1 June. Try them in the demo.

The demo lets you try two changes. Waiting for the status check NPCI allows at ninety seconds, before asking for another payment, cuts customers charged twice in June from 24 to 9, but during the slowdown the tills spend 99 minutes taking payment instead of 77, exactly when the queue is longest. Putting the bill ID on every UPI request doesn’t stop double charges, but every one is matched the next morning and refunded: the median wait falls from 4.9 days to 0.7, nothing is left in suspense, and complaints fall from 26 to 12. A payment that carries a reference to what it is for can always be matched to it.
