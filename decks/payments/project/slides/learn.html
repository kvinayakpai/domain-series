How to learn this domain

How UPI moves money

The parties in a payment: app, NPCI, the customer’s bank, the merchant’s bank, the provider. Then NPCI’s 2017 circular on deemed approval.

Cards, for contrast

Glenbrook, Payments Systems in the U.S.: authorisation, clearing, settlement and the four-party model.

When payments fail

RBI’s 2019 circular on turnaround times and compensation; NPCI’s 2025 circulars on status checks and response times.

Reconciliation

The keys that join payment records: RRN, the merchant’s reference, settlement dates. Credits with no sale; sales with no credit.

Practice

Download June’s payment records and find every customer who paid twice.

A rough plan for a few evenings a week over about two months. Links in the post: kvinayakpai.github.io/domain-series/payments
