NPCI UPI and Services Steering Committee, reported by Deccan Chronicle (15 Sep 2026) and Business Standard (16 Sep 2026).

Until this month UPI payments to merchants from bank accounts carried no merchant discount rate. From the fifteenth of October 2026, payments to a merchant above two thousand rupees carry 0.4 percent, capped at three hundred rupees, and small merchants receiving up to a lakh a month through UPI QR are exempt. For Store 014, June’s bills would have cost about ₹8,082. The shape matters more than the size: the fee applies to the whole payment once it crosses two thousand, so a basket of 2,001 rupees costs eight rupees to accept and a basket of 2,000 costs nothing.
