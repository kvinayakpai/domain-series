As in the other posts, nobody here is careless, and each data product reads the records in front of it. The payments dashboard shows approvals at the tills: 95.9% of June’s UPI attempts, falling to 69% and 67% in the 18:00 and 19:00 hours on Saturday 20 June and back to normal by eight, so the incident is closed. The data tests pass, all ten: every bill has exactly one approved payment, every UPI payment the till approved is a success in the provider’s report, and the settlements add up. The reconciliation report matches the provider’s credits to the till’s approvals by reference number, and parks the 17 credits with no approval behind them in suspense. Customer support takes 23 calls from people charged twice and asks for screenshots. And an AI assistant, given the till’s schema, says nobody paid twice: all 30,077 bills have exactly one approved payment.
