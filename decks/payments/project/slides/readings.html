Nobody here is careless. The cashier sees a screen that says failed and takes another payment, as every cashier does: 30 times in ninety minutes. Payments operations sees approvals fall to 69% and 67% for two hours and recover, and closes the incident. The reconciliation analyst finds 17 UPI credits with no approved payment behind them and parks them in suspense. Customer support takes 23 calls from people charged twice and asks for screenshots. Finance sees UPI settle with no fees and small refunds.
